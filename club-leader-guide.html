Why should my club have a group on Grandview Exchange?

Having a group on Grandview Exchange saves you time and simplifies communication. It eliminates the need for you to maintain personal email lists, allows you to easily share leadership duties with others, and automatically syncs your club's events with the main Community calendar. Plus, the system handles sending event reminders to your members automatically!

How does it work? It sounds complicated.

It works very much like you communicate today.

  • You send messages to club members by sending an email to your Grandview Exchange group email address.
  • You add events to your club's Grandview Exchange group calendar just like adding events to your own (Apple, Google, Outlook) calendar

How do I get my club on Grandview Exchange?

Getting started is simple! Reach out to the community administrator (me), and I will create a dedicated group page for your club. Once the group is created, we can begin adding your members.

Do club members have to be on Grandview Exchange?

Yes, members need to be added to your club's group on Grandview Exchange to receive communications and calendar invites. However, they do not need to be active website users to benefit—they can interact entirely through their regular email.

Can the club members and I keep using email?

Absolutely! You can send messages to the group directly from your preferred email client (like Outlook or Gmail) using the group's unique email address. When members reply, it goes to the whole group, just like a standard mailing list.

How do I send an email to club members?

Simply send an email to your club's unique Grandview Exchange email address. (When your club's group is set up, you will receive your unique email address.)

How do I add events to the calendar?

  1. Navigate to your club's group page on Grandview Exchange.
  2. Click on the Calendar link.
  3. Click Add Event (or click on the specific date of your activity).
  4. Fill in the event details (Title, Date, Time, and Location).
  5. Click Save. (Note: This will automatically notify your members and sync the event to the main Community calendar!)

For more detailed instructions, including a visual guide, click this link: Visual Guide to Add Calendar Events

How do I add and remove members for my group on Grandview Exchange?

To keep things easy for you, simply send me the names and email addresses of new members, and I will add them to your group. If someone leaves the club, just let me know, and I will remove them for you. (If you are interested, I can show you how to do this yourself. It's easy.)

Logging in to Grandview Exchange Using Your Browser.

When you access Grandview Exchange in your browser, you may have to log in if it is the first time you are visiting or if you have logged out during your previous visit. If you see Login in the upper right corner of the page instead of your name, you need to log in. Follow the steps below:

Logging in with Just Your Email Address (Recommended)

You can log into your Grandview Exchange Message Boards account by just using your email address. You don't need to remember yet another password.

  1. Click the light blue Email me a link to log in button.
  2. On the Send Log In Link page, enter your Email Address.
  3. Click the greeen Email me a link to log in button.
  4. You will receive an email from Groups.io Notifications. Click the Log In link in the email.
  5. Your browser will open Grandview Exchange and automatically log you in.

Logging in with Your Email Address and Password

If you prefer the old-fashioned email address and password approach to logging in, we've got you covered.

  1. Click the Login link in the upper right corner of the page.
  2. Enter your Email Address and Password.
  3. Click the green Log In with Passwordbutton.

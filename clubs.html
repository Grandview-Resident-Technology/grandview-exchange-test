Grandview Clubs

The Grandview Bethesda has a thriving network of clubs that continues to grow. See the list below for details.

Don't see a club for your interests? Start one yourself! Contact Resident Services at 301-272-9654 to get started. Once your new club is formed, contact the Grandview Exchange Administrator to set up a subgroup on Grandview Exchange. Being on Grandview Exchange makes it easy to send messages to club members, coordinate activities with a group calendar, and more!

Bocce Club

The Bocce Club is designed to bring residents together, foster connections, and create memorable experiences through fun and exercise. We invite residents of all ages, from 60 to 100, to join us in this excellent venture. Participate as much or as little as you'd like - your involvement is always your choice. But we hope to “See you on the court!”
  • Club Leader: Steve Lodge
  • Bocce Club Subgroup on Grandview Exchange

Bridge Club

The Bridge Club is designed to bring residents together, foster connections, and create memorable experiences through playing bridge. With our tagline, “Let’s Play!,” we invite residents of all ages—ranging from 60 to 100—to join us in this excellent venture. Participate as much or as little as you’d like—your involvement is always your choice.
  • Club Leader: Barbara Stern
  • Bridge Club Subgroup on Grandview Exchange

Computer/IT Club

The Computer/IT Club, created in July 2023, was one of the first Grandview clubs. As of September 2026, we have 57 members. Meetings of the Club are held bi-monthy. The Club is a forum for members to become more knowledgeable about the use of their electronic devices – such as computers, laptops, tablets (like iPads), and smartphones. The Club’s members range from novices on tech issues to people who have been in the tech business or who have been speakers on tech and AI topics at local senior educational centers, such as the Osher Life-Long Learning Institutes at American University and Johns Hopkins.
  • Club Leader: Sheldon Hochberg
  • Computer/IT Club Subgroup on Grandview Exchange

Current Events Club

The Current Events Club is designed to bring residents together and help foster connections through stimulating discussions on topics of the day. The Grandview is filled with residents from the DC area and from around the globe with varied and fascinating backgrounds — government service, the media, World Bank, politics, non-profits, education and the legal and healthcare communities. We have found that many residents are interested in discussing world events, sharing their expertise and learning from each other.
  • Club Leader: Elizabeth Mclean

Fiction Book Clubs

The fiction book clubs are made up of participants, led by a facilitator, who value open discussion, respect for differing opinions, and are open to reading a variety of books from short story anthologies, to best sellers, to classics, to whatever piques our interest in the world of fiction. We will collectively select books to read; one for each month, several months in advance.

There are multiple fiction book clubs at The Grandview. Club membership is so popular, the club leaders decided to form multiple clubs with a maximum of 15 members. To join a club, please join the Fiction Book Lobby, which allows residents to express interest in joining the next available club.

  • Fiction Book Club Lobby Subgroup on Grandview Exchange

The Grand Club

The Grand Club is a welcoming group of Grandview residents (men and women) interested in discussing relevant topics impacting on them, and also hearing interesting community speakers. The Club was formed several months ago and meets on the 12th floor for its programs and has also sponsored outstanding speakers of interest to all residents in the Enrichment Center. Please join the Club and its mailing list, so you will be informed of all Club programs and forthcoming speakers. The Club encourages active participation and welcomes speaker nominations.
  • Club Leader: Charles Hellery

The Grandview Jewish Congregation

The Grandview Jewish Congregation fosters a welcoming, spiritually vibrant, and inclusive Jewish community for the residents of The Grandview Senior Living in Bethesda, Maryland. Rooted in Jewish tradition and guided by the values of kehillah (community), chesed (kindness), and tikkun olam (repairing the world), we provide meaningful worship, lifelong learning, pastoral support, and opportunities for connection. We welcome residents of all backgrounds, levels of observance, and abilities. Our mission is to enrich the lives of residents through accessible, uplifting, and compassionate religious and communal experiences, including Shabbat and holiday services.

  • Contact: Ron Myers

History Club

The History Club is a friendly place for anyone who enjoys exploring the stories, people, and events that shaped our world. Whether your interests lean toward ancient civilizations, American history, world cultures, famous leaders, local Montgomery County history, or anything in between, you’ll find curious neighbors here who love learning and sharing.

Mah Jongg Club

Mah Jongg is more than just a game—it's a wonderful way to keep the mind active and give those brain cells a little rejuvenation each week. Just as importantly, it's an opportunity to build camaraderie, enjoy good conversation, and strengthen friendships with neighbors around the table. Come join the fun and festivities at The Grandview. Pull up a chair, shuffle the tiles, and let the games begin!

  • Club Leader: Alice Brauer
  • Mah Jongg Club Subgroup on Grandview Exchange

Modern American Canasta

Good friends, good times, good canasta! We invite residents of all ages to join in on the fun. Play as often as you like, your involvement is your choice. We are playing by the rules endorsed by the Canasta League of America (CLA). Whether you are a very experienced player or just learning the game, we welcome everyone to come and play. We help new players learn the game and enjoy the experience. We foster connection, friendship, camaraderie and most of all fun. Join us in building a community where friendships flourish and shared interests bring us closer together. Together, lets make Modern American Canasta a place where everyone feels welcome and at home.

  • Club Leaders: Judy Zabell, Jim Silva
  • Modern American Canasta Subgroup on Grandview Exchange

Non-Fiction Book Club

Our non-fiction book club has been going for over 2 years, so we have read a variety of books; one thing that is astonishing to all of us is how interesting many books were that we would have never dreamed of picking up to read, were it not for the club. A few of my surprise favorites: The Art Thief, Pathogenesis, Mme Fourcades Secret War, The Wager. So far, we have been able to meet in person; if the club grows we may divide into 2 groups. You are welcome, even if you have not read the book; we always enjoy another member.

  • Club Leader: Susan Shuck
  • Non-Fiction Book Club subgroup on Grandview Exchange

Photography Club

The Photography Club is a place for residents who enjoy taking pictures, learning new techniques, and sharing their creative work. Members use this subgroup to post photos, ask questions, explore equipment, and connect with others who love capturing the world through a lens.

Pickleball Club

Welcome to the Grandview Pickleball Club, open to everyone, ages 60 to 100, with the simple motto: “Play Pickle!.” Whether you're an experienced player or a beginner, you can play at your own pace and enjoy a fun, supportive community. Members are always happy to assist and ensure everyone can participate - from teaching some basics, running practice drills or organizing games.

Romance Languages Club

The Romance Languages Club aims to foster a welcoming environment where members can enjoy speaking, listening, and exchanging ideas in Spanish, French, and Italian sub-groups. The focus is on practice and fluency, not formal instruction, as members have a basic working knowledge of at least one of the languages.
  • Club Leader: Margaret Myers

Social Singles Group

The Singles Social Group (or the Social Singles Group) is designed to bring single residents together, foster connections, and create memorable experiences through fun and social interaction. We recognize that people without a partner have different outlooks, interests, and support needs. Singles Social Group is the perfect place to explore, learn, and connect with others with similar lifestyles.
  • Club Leader: Jan Augustine
  • Social Singles Group subgroup on Grandview Exchange

Speakers Program Steering Committee

The Speakers Program Steering Committee hosts thought-provoking speakers each month for Grandview residents and guests on topics of interest to the community for educational and cultural goals. Our vision is to enhance the quality of life at the Grandview with a monthly gathering, where every member feels welcome, becomes better informed and continues learning.

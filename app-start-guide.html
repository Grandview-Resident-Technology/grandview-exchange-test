Choose the app if you want quick access from your home screen, a simple mobile‑friendly layout, and notifications when new messages arrive.

  • Quick access from your home screen
  • A simple, mobile‑friendly layout
  • Notifications when new messages arrive
  • An easy way to read and post on the go

Installing the App

The Groups.io app is available for download on Apple and Android devices.

You can install it using the links below:

  • Apple App Store (for iPhone and iPad)
  • Google Play Store (for Android devices)

Once installed, open the app and sign in with your Grandview Exchange account.

  • If you don't have an account yet, click this link to register.
  • If you need help logging in, click this link for assistance.

Understanding the App Main Screen

When you log into the app for the first time, you will see the list of the groups you have joined along with a brief description of the group. At the bottom, you will see buttons you can tap to access frequently used features. The button on the right (showing three horizontal lines and the word More) opens a menu where you can access additional features. (On tablets with larger screens, you will see links on the left side of the page instead of the buttons on the bottom.)


Tap on the group you want to view and you will see the Messages page showing all messages posted in that group.

Understanding the Messages Page

The Messages page shows all recent posts in the group, with the most recent message at the top. This is where you’ll spend most of your time when using Grandview Exchange in the app.

Posting a New Message

To share something with the community:

  • Click the New Topic button at the bottom of the page. (If you're using a tablet, the link may be on the left side of the page.)
  • Type a short, clear subject line
  • Write your message
  • Add photos or files if you want
  • Click Post to publish your message

Reading Messages

To read any message:

  • Click the subject line to open it
  • Scroll to read the full text and any replies

If the message is part of a longer conversation, you’ll see earlier replies below it.

Replying to a Message

To reply to a message:

  • Open the message
  • Click the Reply link below the message
  • Normally, replies are sent only to the person who posted the message. To reply to the group, click on the box below your name and choose "Reply to Group
  • Enter your reply in the box below the subject line
  • Click the Send button near the bottom.

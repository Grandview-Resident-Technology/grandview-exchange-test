Is the Browser a Good Choice for You?

Choose the browser if you want to see more on the screen at one time and prefer the familiarity of a web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).

  • A familiar way to access web sites on a computer or tablet
  • Easier to see more information at once
  • Requires using links or using bookmarks to get to the site

Opening Grandview Exchange in Your Browser

You can use your favorite browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) on any device.

Click this link to go to Grandview Exchange in your browser, or open your browser and enter grandviewexchange.groups.io.

If you want to make it easy to get back to the site, you can create a bookmark or favorite in your Browser.

  • If you want to create a bookmark or favorite, follow these instructions.

Once Grandview Exchange opens in your browser, you may need to log in with your Grandview Exchange account.

  • If you don't have an account yet, click this link to register.
  • If you need help logging in, click this link for assistance.

Once you log in, you will see the Grandview Exchange Home Page.

Understanding the Home Page

The Grandview Exchange Home Page is the main screen you see after logging in. It includes helpful information about the community and clear links that guide you to the most important parts of the site. This page is your starting point for reading messages, exploring club subgroups, and managing how you receive email.

On the left side of the screen, you will see a list of links. Below is an exaplanation of the links you will use most often:

  • Subscription — Lets you choose how many emails you receive from Grandview Exchange and create a profile to tell other members about yourself.
  • Messages — This is where you go to read, post, and reply to messages.
  • Subgroups — Lists all club groups at The Grandview. Each subgroup has its own space for club discussions, updates, photos, and announcements.

Understanding the Messages Page

The Messages page shows all recent posts in the Home Group, with the most recent message at the top. This is where you’ll spend most of your time when using Grandview Exchange in a browser.

Posting a New Message

To share something with the community:

  • Click the New Topic link on the left side of the page
  • Type a short, clear subject line
  • Write your message
  • Add photos or files if you want
  • Click Post to publish your message

Reading Messages

To read any message:

  • Click the subject line to open it
  • Scroll to read the full text and any replies

If the message is part of a longer conversation, you’ll see earlier replies below it.

Replying to a Message

To reply to a message:

  • Open the message
  • Click the Reply link below the message
  • Normally, replies are sent only to the person who posted the message. To reply to the group, click on the box below your name and choose "Reply to Group
  • Enter your reply in the box below the subject line
  • Click the Send button near the bottom.

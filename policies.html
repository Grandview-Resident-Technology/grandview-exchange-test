Community Rules for Grandview Exchange Members

The Grandview Exchange is intended to foster connection, communication, and collaboration among residents in our Grandview community.

Navigating, viewing, or otherwise using Grandview Exchange constitutes full acceptance of these community rules. Continued use following any rule updates serves as your consent to the revised terms. If you do not accept these terms, you must discontinuing using Grandview Exchange and delete your account.

Community Rules Overview

To maintain a welcoming and respectful environment, we’ve developed these Community Rules to ensure that every member of Grandview Exchange knows what is expected of them and feels comfortable participating.

By joining Grandview Exchange (i.e., becoming a Member), you hereby:

  • Confirm that you are a resident, have reserved an apartment (i.e., are a reservist), or are on the Priority List of The Grandview Bethesda, and that you have read and understood these Community Rules.
  • Agree to abide by these Community Rules in practice and in spirit, including any future amendments, which will be made available on Grandview Exchange’s Wiki (accessible via the Wiki link in the navigation menu).
  • Take full responsibility for anything you do or post using Grandview Exchange facilities (including the Grandview Exchange Home Group, subgroups, and related resources).
  • Recognize that your continued access to Grandview Exchange facilities may be limited or revoked as a result of repeated or severe violations of these Community Rules.

Furthermore, you acknowledge that:

  • Certain personal information about you (e.g., email address, posted messages that may contain personal details such as activities and interests) will be used as part of the operation of Grandview Exchange.
  • The Administrator and volunteer members endeavor to maintain a secure environment for all information on Grandview Exchange, but cannot guarantee absolute privacy of information you make available.
  • You agree to resolve, respectfully and helpfully, any dispute or disagreement arising out of the operation of Grandview Exchange. Unresolved disputes will be finally resolved exclusively through a complaint submitted to the Grandview Exchange Governance Council [to be established].

This document consists of three main sections:

  • General Community Rules members must follow.
  • Community Rules for posting messages on Grandview Exchange.
  • Privacy Policies of Grandview Exchange.

General Community Rules

Purpose of Grandview Exchange

Grandview Exchange is intended solely for communication, information sharing, and community building among Grandview Exchange Members. It is not a forum for third parties outside of the Grandview Community. Additionally, Grandview Exchange is not an official channel for submitting, discussing, or coordinating requests or grievances to Grandview management, which does not sponsor and has not been involved in its creation.

Standards of Behavior

In all matters regarding Grandview Exchange, members must be respectful and considerate of other members. Do not engage in name-calling or use disparaging, abusive, or offensive language against any individual, group, or entity—including other members, Grandview residents, and Grandview staff.

Commercial Activity Prohibited

Use of Grandview Exchange for commercial activity is not permitted. This includes advertising or soliciting services for profit or offering to sell items not owned by the Member.

Community Rules for Posting Messages

No Controversial or Divisive Topics

Avoid posting or participating in discussions that may be polarizing or provoke conflict or discomfort for other members—including partisan politics, differences in religious views, racism, sexism, or any form of discrimination. Our goal is to focus on what brings us together, not what might divide us.

Grandview Exchange is not a debating society. It will be more valuable to members if we avoid debate about provocative or divisive topics. Other forums or listservs are available for discussing such matters.

No Requests or Grievances to Management

Do not post messages intended to submit, discuss, or coordinate requests, complaints, or grievances directed toward Grandview management. Grandview Exchange is an independent community forum and is not an official channel for communicating with management, which does not monitor or sponsor this site. Please direct all official requests or service issues to Grandview management directly through official channels.

Focus on Positive Engagement

Use Grandview Exchange to share helpful information, plan events, ask for recommendations or advice, or connect with fellow members. If you are unsure whether your content is appropriate, ask yourself: "Does this contribute positively to the community? Is this respectful to everyone, regardless of their beliefs or background?"

Zero Tolerance for Offensive Behavior

Posts containing offensive language, personal attacks, or any form of harassment are strictly prohibited.

No Impersonation

Do not impersonate any person or entity, or misrepresent your affiliation with a person or entity.

Avoid Defamatory Statements

Statements about an individual or business (such as a local restaurant) that are false or contain knowingly erroneous information should never be made. If you express a negative opinion about an individual or business, you must avoid false information and bear full responsibility for the consequences of your message.

No Spam or Unlawful Messages

Do not post promotional materials, "junk mail," "spam," chain letters, pyramid schemes, or messages that violate Federal, Maryland, or Montgomery County laws or regulations.

Attachments

Do not attach any document, photo, or content to a message that:

  • Violates intellectual property or proprietary rights of any party.
  • You do not have the legal or contractual right to share.
  • Contains viruses, malware, or code designed to harm computer functionality.
  • Creates a privacy or security risk to any person.

No Data Harvesting & Keep Messages Local

No Data Harvesting: Do not collect email addresses, personal information of members, or content posted on this site.

Keep Messages Local: Messages should be relevant to our community and focus on local topics, events, and interests.

Your Privacy

Reliance on Groups.io Core Policies

Grandview Exchange is hosted on the Groups.io platform. Fundamental aspects of data collection, storage, and protection are governed by the Official Groups.io Privacy Policy.

Unlike Google Groups or Facebook Groups, Groups.io does not use your postings for advertising data, build marketing profiles, or track your activities. It is a subscription-based service. Your email addresses and group content remain private and are not shared.

Groups.io Data Collection & Cookie Policy

Groups.io collects login information (name and email address) and uses cookies to operate its service according to industry-standard safeguards. For details on cookie types and browser controls, consult the Groups.io Cookie Policy. Participation is also subject to the Groups.io Terms of Service.

Grandview Exchange Privacy Considerations

  • Information You Share: Any content you upload or post is your responsibility. Exercise caution when sharing sensitive personal information.
  • Member Responsibilities: Respect the privacy of others. Do not share personal information of another person without their consent.
  • Restricted Membership Subgroups: Grandview Clubs may set up restricted subgroups with additional privacy terms. Joining a Club subjects you to those specific terms.
  • Third-Party Services: Neither Groups.io nor Grandview Exchange are responsible for third-party services (e.g., if you use Gmail, Google handles your email according to its own privacy policy).

Enforcement

Grandview Exchange moderators (who are volunteers) endeavor to monitor messages to ensure these Community Rules are honored. Messages violating these rules may be prohibited before posting or removed after posting. Members who repeatedly violate rules will be contacted privately and may be temporarily or permanently removed.

If you see a message that violates these rules, please report it to the Administrator at home+owner@grandviewexchange.groups.io. Your report will be handled discreetly and with care.

Together, we believe Grandview Exchange can create a supportive, helpful, and enjoyable online experience where every member benefits.

Thank you for becoming a Grandview Exchange member. Let’s be kind, stay connected, and build something extraordinary. 🌟
